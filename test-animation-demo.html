Speed Control

Frame: 0 | Base delay: 100ms | Adjusted: 100ms | FPS: 10
How this relates to GIFs:
• Tenor GIFs have ~100ms delays between frames (10 FPS)
• Speed 3x = 33ms delays (30 FPS) - good for Slack
• Speed 5x = 20ms delays (50 FPS) - very snappy
• The ball represents a GIF frame updating at the adjusted speed
